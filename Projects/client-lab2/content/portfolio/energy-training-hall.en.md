{{< energy-step number="01" title="The first concept" image="energy-training-hall-process-01.png" alt="First Energy Training Hall logo concept: the 力 mark and horizontal lockups on black" caption="The first brief-led direction, shown as a symbol and horizontal lockups." zoomLabel="Open image at full size" >}}
The work began with the logo brief. The first concept centred on 力, a character associated with strength. Sharp lines, a high-contrast black, white and red palette, and a sense of movement gave the identity its athletic character.
{{< /energy-step >}}

{{< energy-step number="02" title="Finding the form" image="energy-training-hall-process-02.png" alt="A sheet of simplified 力 symbol variations and horizontal Energy Training Hall logo layouts" caption="Explorations of the symbol and horizontal composition." zoomLabel="Open image at full size" >}}
Next, I began simplifying the mark. I tested the character’s proportions, the curve of its strokes and the placement of the wordmark. The aim was to keep the sense of motion, make the logo more concise and find a composition that would work across different formats.
{{< /energy-step >}}

{{< energy-step number="03" title="Bringing in the athlete" image="energy-training-hall-process-03.png" alt="Two early logo options integrating an athlete into the 力 character" caption="Initial explorations of the athlete as part of the character." zoomLabel="Open image at full size" >}}
During the process, the client suggested adding a human figure. The challenge was to combine an athlete’s silhouette with the character without losing the symbol’s recognisable shape. I explored ways for the figure to become part of the composition rather than a separate decorative element.
{{< /energy-step >}}

{{< energy-step number="04" title="Developing the silhouette" image="energy-training-hall-process-03.png" alt="The same two athlete logo explorations, reviewed for silhouette and movement" caption="Comparing the pose and the way the figure works with the symbol." zoomLabel="Open image at full size" >}}
I then focused on the figure’s movement. I adjusted the pose and proportions, and refined how the silhouette interacted with the character. Gradually, the composition became more unified: the athlete’s movement began to reinforce the shape and meaning of the mark.
{{< /energy-step >}}

{{< energy-step number="05" title="The final identity" image="energy-training-hall-process-04.png" alt="Final Energy Training Hall logo: standalone mark and horizontal wordmark with the athlete lifting a dumbbell" caption="The final symbol and horizontal logo lockup." zoomLabel="Open image at full size" >}}
The final version combines 力 with an athlete lifting a weight. The white silhouette gives the mark strong contrast, while a red accent adds movement and energy. The identity includes both a standalone mark and a horizontal lockup for use across different formats.
{{< /energy-step >}}
